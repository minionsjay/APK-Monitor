=== PSK重大突破 ===

发现：30138端口forwarder control用TLS PSK认证！

证据(from strings):
1. "forwarderControlPSK" → PSK配置
2. "initPskExt" → 初始化PSK TLS扩展
3. "psk_dhe_ke" → PSK+DHE密钥交换
4. "psk_id_hash" → PSK identity hash
5. "unknown PSK identity" → 服务端验证PSK identity
6. "empty control psk" → 空的PSK错误
7. "empty device uuid" → device_uuid为空错误
8. "hello too large" → hello消息错误
9. "write hello: %w" → 写hello
10. "read length: %w" → 读长度
11. "parse app line ips json: %w" → 解析app_line_ips!
12. "decode response mac: %w" → 验证响应MAC
13. "app_line_update" → app_line更新

结论:
- 30138端口用TLS PSK(不是标准TLS)
- PSK identity = device_uuid
- PSK密钥 = AES_key的某种派生
- 发送JSON返回EOF因为没有PSK认证
- 需要在TLS ClientHello中包含PSK扩展

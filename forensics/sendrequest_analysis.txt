=== sendRequestData分析 ===

sendRequestData流程:
1. writeInt64(?) → 写auth_key_id?
2. writeInt64(msg_id)
3. writeInt32(msg_len)
4. serializeToStream → constructor+nonce
5. type==2 → getPermanentKey / type!=2 → getTempKey

sendRequest(0xdd2e08)中的加密:
1. encryptKeyWithSecret(this, data+0x28c, type) ← 第一次
2. hash(data+0x28c, 256) → 0x89f158
3. 从data[7..55]读8字节 → auth_key_id
4. encryptKeyWithSecret(this, data+0x28c, type) ← 第二次
5. hash(data+0x28c, 256) → 0x89f158
6. bl 0x89f320(data, 256, 64, ...) → HMAC?

关键: encryptKeyWithSecret加密256字节块
data+0x28c = 256字节的数据缓冲区

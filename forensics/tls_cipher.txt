=== TLS cipher对比 ===

APP: cipher=0xc013 (TLS_ECDHE_RSA_WITH_AES_128_CBC_SHA)
标准crypto/tls: cipher=0xc02f (TLS_ECDHE_RSA_WITH_AES_128_GCM_SHA256)
utls(指定0xc013): cipher=0xc013

结果:
  标准crypto/tls(0xc02f) → EOF
  utls(0xc013) → timeout或EOF
  APP(0xc013) → 成功

结论:
  cipher不是关键区别
  可能APP不发送明文req_pq
  可能用永久auth_key加密后发送DH握手
